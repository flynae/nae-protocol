NOVEMBER 2026

What a risk assessment actually asks

Likelihood, impact, and which assets matter most. Tap to read more.

Read post

On our capstone, we started by listing what Akwaaba needs to protect: the website, web server, database, operating system, admin accounts, and people. For each, we asked how likely a compromise is and how bad it would be.

The takeaway: exposure drives priority. The public-facing site rose to the top, while internal systems mattered because one stolen admin credential could reach all of them.
